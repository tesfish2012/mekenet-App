2. Store listing

Short description

Maximum 80 characters:

Mekenet Insurance — manage policies, claims and payments on the go

Full description

Mekenet Insurance makes it easy to manage your insurance services from your mobile device.

With Mekenet Insurance, customers can conveniently access their insurance information and manage their policies wherever they are.

Key features:

View and manage insurance policies
Access policy details and coverage information
Submit and manage insurance claims
Track claim status
Access your digital insurance card
View insurance-related payments and information
Receive important notifications and updates
Access broker and insurance service functionality
Manage your account securely

Mekenet Insurance provides a convenient digital experience for accessing insurance services and staying informed about your policies and claims.
